Undo/Redo Editor

Type, delete or paste anywhere in the text. Every edit is pushed onto a stack, and undo pops the most recent one first.

Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z to redo.
